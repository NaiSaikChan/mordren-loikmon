### Description (max 4000)

```
Loikmon is a digital home for Mon literature. Read Mon eBooks, listen to audiobooks, and discover articles and authors, wherever you are.

READ IN COMFORT
• Open EPUB and PDF books in a reader designed for long sessions
• Light, sepia, dark and black reading themes
• Adjust font, font size and line spacing to suit your eyes
• Jump between chapters with the table of contents
• Pick up where you left off, even on another device
• Download books and keep reading offline

LISTEN ANYWHERE
• Audiobooks play in the background and from the lock screen
• A mini player follows you around the app
• Playback speed from 0.75× to 2×
• Skip back or forward 15 seconds
• Sleep timer: 5 to 60 minutes, or stop at the end of the chapter
• Try preview chapters before you subscribe

DISCOVER
• Featured titles, the latest books and new articles on the home screen
• Browse by category and search the whole catalogue
• Follow the authors you love
• Read reviews and find related books
• Save titles to your personal library

MADE FOR MON READERS
• Use the app in English or in Mon
• Choose the body and heading fonts for Mon script
• Dark mode, and layouts that fit both phones and iPad

LOIKMON PREMIUM
One subscription unlocks every eBook, every article and every audiobook chapter. Articles show an excerpt and audiobooks include preview chapters for everyone.

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription, with 1-month, 3-month, 6-month and 12-month plans. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency.

• Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase.
• The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
• Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
• You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings after purchase.

Terms of Use: https://app.loikmon.org/policies/terms
Privacy Policy: https://app.loikmon.org/policies/privacy
```
